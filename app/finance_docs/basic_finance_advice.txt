
            # Basic Financial Advice
            
            ## Budgeting
            Create a monthly budget that tracks income and expenses. Aim to save at least 20% of your income.
            
            ## Emergency Fund
            Build an emergency fund that covers 3-6 months of expenses. Keep this in a liquid, easily accessible account.
            
            ## Debt Management
            Pay off high-interest debt first, such as credit cards. Consider the debt avalanche or debt snowball methods.
            
            ## Investments
            For long-term goals, consider investing in a diversified portfolio of stocks and bonds. In India, options include mutual funds, PPF, NPS, and ELSS for tax benefits.
            
            ## Tax Planning
            Utilize Section 80C deductions through investments in PPF, ELSS, and insurance premiums. Consider Section 80D for health insurance premiums.
            
            ## Retirement Planning
            Start early for retirement planning. NPS and EPF are good options in India. Aim to save at least 15% of your income for retirement.
